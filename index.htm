FCS Validator

Validate FCS file compliance against the corresponding specification. Supports FCS versions 2.0, 3.0, 3.1 and 3.2.

This open-source tool is developed and maintained by the CellEngine team at CellCarta. See the code, discuss and open issues at https://github.com/cellengine/fcs-validator/.

Validation runs in your browser without uploading anything to the internet.
